# Space Janitors 
###  A basic game where you cleanup space junk to upgrade your rocket 

Space Janitors is a simple yet fun game inspired by the original Atari Asteroids but with a few modern twists I came up with.

# QUICK START
### Requirements 
- Any PC browser
- A keyboard
- A mouse

### How to start playing

On your browser go to https://bawad2.itch.io/space-janitors. It should Start the game automatically.

### Controls
- for navigating through the map **click on the icons that are highlighted** when you hover over them.
- When in a shop menu click on the buttons to buy upgrades or the cross button to go back to the game.
- When you make to the rocket level use **Scroll wheel** or **W and S** to control the speed of the rocket.
- press **A and D** to control the tilt of the rocket and dictate where it goes.
- **Left click** for shooting bullets.

### What to do when inside the game.

First thing you do is **go outside** by clicking on the **arrow near the door**. From outside you can click on the house to go back to the house or **click on the car** to go to the rocket launch site. In the rocket launch site you can click on the **Research lab to buy upgrades** with coins *(shown in the counter in the top left corner of the screen)*. After that you can **click on the rocket** to finally start piloting it. In the first rocket room **increase your throttle** to fly through the top of the room. In the second room beware of the thunder clouds because they deal damage *(they can be broken if you shoot them tho)*. After the 2nd room you finally make it to space. When in space you get to **break the infinitely spawning asteroids** unless you *run out of fuel or health*. Each asteroid you break gives you a set amount of coins which you can use later to upgrade your rocket. When the game ends you **click on the next day button** and repeat.
